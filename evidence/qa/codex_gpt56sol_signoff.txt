## Exact coverage map

The hash chain covers:

- `entries[i].sequence`
- the complete nested `entries[i].entry`
- `entries[i].previous_hash`
- non-null `entries[i].authorized_by`
- `entry_hash` indirectly, by recomputation

It does **not** cover `source_entry_hash`, `agent_signature`, arbitrary additional wrapper fields, or the distinction between 
absent and explicitly null `authorized_by`.

The operator signature covers:

- `session.session_id`
- `merkle_root`
- recomputed `len(entries)`
- `session.closed_at`
- `schema_version`
- `session.agent_fingerprint`
- constant `"RFC8785"`

Consequently, top-level `version`, extra top-level fields, `org_signature.signed_at`, `source_entry_hash`, and wrapper extras 
are not operator-signed. Entries are indirectly protected through the recomputed chain, Merkle root, and signed root.

At session level, these are **not** operator-signed or chain-covered:

- `org_id`
- `agent_id`, `agent_version`, `agent_public_key`, `signature_scheme`
- `environment`
- `initiated_by`, `initiated_at`
- `compliance_contexts`, `policy_context`
- `human_authorizer`
- `intent_contract`, `contract_hash`
- `retention_days`
- `metadata`

`agent_public_key` is only checked operationally against signatures when it is present; deleting it does not invalidate an 
otherwise valid log.

For `evidence_record.json`, none of its six top-level projections is hash-chain-covered. Exact bytes are conditionally 
protected by `manifest.files{}` and the manifest signature, but the current verifier does not require or identity-bind that 
signature. Only `operations[].served_model` receives an independent projection comparison, and currently that comparison uses 
unverified NDJSON.

 343:     *,
 344:     mode: str = "full",
 345:     sign: bool = True,
 346: ) -> dict[str, str]:
 347:     """Write a bundle to *out_dir* and return ``{filename: sha256-of-bytes}``.
 348:

 352:     ``hash_manifest.json`` LAST. The manifest's ``files{}`` map holds the SHA-256

 354:     ``sign=True`` (default) the manifest is operator-sealed over the JCS of the
 355:     manifest minus ``operator_signature``.

 365:     log = _assemble_log(store, session, entries, redacted=redacted, sign=not redacted)
